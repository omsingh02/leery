# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Leery is a game people play with friends, and this project should be just as pleasant to take part in. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity, experience level or
nationality.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps build a good community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Owning mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people playing it

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory language
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing other people's private information without permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

The maintainer, [@omsingh02](https://github.com/omsingh02), is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow them, or ban people who
repeatedly or seriously violate them.

To report a problem, use the [private report form](https://github.com/omsingh02/leery/security/advisories/new). Only the
maintainer can see it. Reports are handled in confidence.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions) and whenever someone represents
the project in public.
